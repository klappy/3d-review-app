# Production briefs — one purposeful video at a time

This revision supersedes the previous 8–10 minute audio/video brief. Follow VIDEO-SERIES.md. Do not combine all scripts into one overview.

## Video 01 — two-minute introduction

Create a single overview no longer than 2:00 including titles and closing card. Follow OVERVIEW-SCRIPT.md. The viewer should leave able to explain that 3D Review assesses implementation, process and team health through translation-team, community and church lenses, and why it complements their existing tools. Use the source document only to verify claims. Do not expand into facilitator training, reporting mechanics, release history or the full series. Use a neutral narrator, captions and readable labels. Time the final cut; a generation length setting alone is not duration evidence.

## Shared evidence and visual constraints

Preserve perceptions of understanding, trust, use and readiness; this is not merely staff satisfaction. Explain that 3D Review does not inspect translated text for errors. Treat checking tools fairly: they can support collaboration and processes too. Compatibility does not claim built-in integrations.

Give three lenses equal visual prominence without implying numerical weighting. Label hypothetical examples. No invented scores, composite grades, field results, customer testimony, partner endorsement, software screenshots, or unverified availability. Neutral narration only, no cloned voice or attribution to klappy. Do not narrate private Bee IDs or include raw conversations. Use captions, readable text and distinctions beyond color.

Any current-release card or real UI footage requires refreshed release evidence. Source-authored interpretation is distinct from new AI-generated interpretation. Review the generated transcript against its script and time the exported asset before accepting it.

## Other overview assets

An introductory audio asset should use the same two-minute script. A short introductory slide set should use five scenes: purpose, three lenses, compatibility, illustrative question, next step. Deeper decks or audio episodes should each follow one follow-up outcome. The full source document remains a research reference, not a narration outline.
