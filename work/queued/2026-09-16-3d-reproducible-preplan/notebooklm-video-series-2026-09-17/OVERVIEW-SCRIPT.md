# 01 — What is 3D Review?

Audience: Prospective users and stakeholders.

Viewer outcome: Explain what 3D Review assesses and decide whether to explore it for your context.

Target duration: 2:00. Spoken words: 217. Timing is a production target, not a verified recording duration.

## Narration

Can you use 3D Review with the Bible translation tools and methods you already use? Yes. Here’s why.

3D Review helps you understand the health of your translation process and team: how your methods are being put into practice, and how people experience the work.

It brings together three lenses: the translation team, the community, and the church.

The translation team can describe preparation, review, revision, and accountability. Community members can share whether they understand, trust, and use the translation. Church participants can speak about involvement, confidence, and use in church life.


3D Review does not directly check your translated text for errors. Keep using translationCore, AQuA, Paratext, or the other tools that support your work. 3D Review helps you ask how those tools and your wider methods are being used, and what people are experiencing. Using them alongside each other does not require a built-in software connection.

For example, imagine a team that feels confident in its review process, while community members describe difficulty understanding the translation. That difference gives the team a useful question to explore. It does not automatically tell you who is right.

The aim is to bring those perspectives into a better-informed conversation about what deserves attention next.

Start with one question: whose perspective would help you understand your translation process better?
