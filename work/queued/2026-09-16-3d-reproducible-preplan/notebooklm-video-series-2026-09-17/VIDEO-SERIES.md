## Viewing paths
Everyone starts with video 01. Then choose the question you need answered: fit → 02; whose voices → 03; preparing participation → 04; interpreting evidence → 05; deciding a next step → 06. No requirement to watch the whole series.

## Two-minute overview timing
Budget approximately 120 words per minute plus short visual pauses. Edit and time the recorded narration to finish within 2:00, including the closing card. If it overruns, shorten narration; do not speed it up to preserve every word. Follow-up timings are estimates and also require a timed read.

## Release updates
