## Failure Modes — What Breaks When Discovery Is Treated as Authorization
- Existence leak: cross-organization matching reveals private names/counts or confirms a hidden target.
- Creation lock: a warning becomes mandatory uniqueness or approval that blocks legitimate creation.
- Scope inheritance: workspace membership is treated as project/assessment permission.
- Unsafe join: matching name/email or requesting access automatically creates a grant.
- Duplicate worker: planning rewrites active NativeAuth/Design implementation or revives rejected PR6.

- Existence leak: remove the information path, challenge indistinguishability and route the exact remaining disclosure choice to Auth before any implementation.

- Scope inheritance: rebind every operation/read to current exact grants and distinguish lawful name-only grouping from content access.
- Unsafe join: keep request/invitation/acceptance/grant states distinct, reuse actual approval boundaries and refuse inferred authority.
- Duplicate worker: stop the overlapping pen, hand the finding to its existing owner and retain this planning return without product edits.
