**Q4 — public list cursor. DISPOSITION: no cursor/pagination helper exists in `src/` (grep `cursor|next_page|page_token|LIMIT` over handlers: none; the only `cursor` is OAuth-provider KV paging, `src/oauth.ts:174–179`; all list handlers return full sets, e.g. `assessment.ts:36`).
