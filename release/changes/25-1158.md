- Added - Print survey can be printed in the participants' language. When the assessment or its project has participant languages, a "Language on paper" choice sits beside Print survey (English first, then the same languages the participant page offers). The questions, answer choices, the passage line and the form's own words come from the same translations the participant page uses; anything not translated yet stays in English and is marked "EN" on the paper. English stays the default, and nothing changes for an assessment without languages.
