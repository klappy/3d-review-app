## [0.22.0] — candidate

- Setup saves on step 1: Continue creates the review as a draft, Home shows it with "Continue setup" (reopens at the next step with what was entered), and Cancel asks in the page whether to keep the draft or discard it. (#339, lane lanes-2311-b06)
