What is 3D Review?

Understand the health of your translation process through three perspectives.

3D Review brings together the translation team, community and church to assess how your methods are being put into practice, how the team is functioning and how people experience the translation. Use it alongside the Bible translation tools and processes you already trust.

Translation team

Understand how the work is carried out: preparation, review, community feedback, revision, consistency and accountability.

Community

Hear how people experience the translation: understanding, use, appeal and trust.

Church

Bring in perspectives on involvement, confidence, affirmation and use.

3D Review is a survey-based assessment of translation-process and team health through three lenses: the translation team, the community and the church. It helps people see where perspectives agree, where they differ and what deserves closer attention.

Together, these perspectives support a fuller conversation. Differences are a reason to investigate, not a reason to hide the evidence in one overall score.

The how: 5 short steps · Go at your own pace

When should I use it? How often?

Use it when your project is ready to pause, reflect and learn from feedback. Repeat when a new assessment would be useful, for example between books or publishing iterations. There is no fixed weekly, monthly or yearly schedule.

    About 3D Review
    3D Review is a survey-based assessment of translation-process and team health through three lenses: the translation team, the community and the church. It helps people see where perspectives agree, where they differ and what deserves closer attention.
    Does 3D Review check our translated text?
    It gathers people's observations about the process, team and translation experience. It does not inspect verses for errors or certify textual accuracy. Continue using the checking and review practices appropriate to your work.
    Does “compatible” mean the apps are connected?
    Compatibility here means complementary use alongside your workflow. A built-in software integration is a separate feature and should be described only where it is actually available.
